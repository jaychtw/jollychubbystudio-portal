Scooter 1945: Taipei Rush

Action · Driving

Ride a red scooter through Taipei’s breakfast rush. Thread between taxis and blue delivery trucks, fire twin headlamp pulses, and save your horn for the garbage truck’s final barrage.

Play Scooter 1945: Taipei Rush
Controls
Steer with arrow keys, WASD, or touch and drag. Use Space or Horn to clear nearby traffic. P or Escape pauses the ride.
