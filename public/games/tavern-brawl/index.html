Tavern Brawl

Action

Shoot bottles, plates, and mugs out of the air while the balcony crowd cheers. Break 20 targets to face the outlaw on Main Street, where three well-timed countershots win the duel.

TB
Play Tavern Brawl
Controls
Move with the left and right arrows or A/D. Press Space to fire and P or Escape to pause. Touch controls are available on screen.
Languages
English, Traditional Chinese
