The Last Crossing

Action · Platformer

Run the rain-slick roofs of two elevated trains above Taipei. Jump the vents and swing across three gaps to reach the evacuation gate in this short 3D proof of concept.

LC
Play The Last Crossing
Controls
Move with WASD or the arrow keys, jump with Space, and press E to tether. Drag to look around.
Languages
English, Traditional Chinese
Before you play
A short proof of concept. Requires a WebGL-enabled browser.
