Shadow Becomes Me

Experimental · Action · Horizontal Scroller

A playable laboratory stealth demo. Become a featureless shadow, disguise yourself as familiar shapes, and escape using Shadow Spike or a silent Shadow Devour.

SB
Play Shadow Becomes Me
Controls
A/D or arrow keys move; C or Transform cycles Shadow → Box → Chair → Shadow. X/Space or Spike triggers the contextual attack. Keyboard, mouse and touch supported.
Languages
English, Traditional Chinese
Before you play
Accepted single-laboratory proof of concept, not a complete campaign. Physical devices, Safari/Firefox and screen readers remain unverified.
