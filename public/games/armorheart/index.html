Armorheart

Action

Four tank missions: open an evacuation route, confront a giant gorilla, intercept a battleship and defeat a fortress. Earn a collection of four cosmetic tanks.

A
Play Armorheart
Controls
Arrows/WASD move and face; hold Space to fire. Touch supports simultaneous direction and Fire. Pause, retry, mute, reduced motion and live language switching.
Languages
English, Traditional Chinese
Before you play
Progress is local to this browser/device. All four tank designs are cosmetic and share identical mechanics. Short landscape screens scroll. Physical-phone, independent first-time-player, other-engine and human assistive-technology tests remain unperformed; full nonvisual Canvas support is incomplete. Accepted fast-win strategies and tank/transport overlap remain. Asset provenance qualifications are retained in the source release preflight.
