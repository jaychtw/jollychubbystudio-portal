The Butterfly Department

Strategy

Move one small thing a day. Watch eight independent lives change. Trace the consequences, rewind time, and discover what might have happened otherwise.

BD
Play The Butterfly Department
Controls
Keyboard, mouse and touch. Select a daily intervention, play/pause, 1×/4×, skip to an event or evening; inspect residents, rewind mornings and compare branches. EN/繁中, fullscreen and optional muted-by-default synthesized sound.
Languages
English, Traditional Chinese
Before you play
Finite authored five-day fictional simulation, not open social AI or real-world psychological advice. Saves are browser-local with export/import; blocked storage keeps session play. Firefox/WebKit, physical devices, OS minimize/restore, assistive technology and independent human aesthetic review are not verified.
