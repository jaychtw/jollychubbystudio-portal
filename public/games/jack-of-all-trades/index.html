Jack of All Trades

Action

A Roman knight survives a ruined arena with two sword attacks and five unlimited spells. Defeat five flaming skulls and a crowned Orc King, then repeat.

JA
Play Jack of All Trades
Controls
A/D or Left/Right move; J/K sword; 1–5 magic; P/Esc pause. Seven visible touch/click actions and two hold-to-move buttons. Live language, mute and fullscreen controls.
Languages
English, Traditional Chinese
Before you play
Mobile play requires landscape. Local best and preferences are browser/device-local; blocked storage or audio degrades gracefully. Repeated Blessing intentionally permits continuous protection. Physical-device, independent human aesthetic and assistive-technology reviews are not performed; full nonvisual Canvas combat is not supported.
