Privacy

Games and apps may save preferences or progress in your browser. Clearing site data removes local copies. Shared features can transmit the information you submit. Embedded media, maps and live streams connect to their providers when loaded. Hosting providers receive connection information when serving pages. Email the studio with privacy questions or to request help with data you shared.
